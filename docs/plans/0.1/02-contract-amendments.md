## 0. How to use this document

**Precedence.** `decisions.md` beats a chapter on any conflict (its own header says so). A chapter beats the contract on behaviour; the contract beats a chapter on names (D-54). This document beats `01-contract.md` and every phase plan wherever they disagree: it is the contract's amendment list, and `01-contract.md` is to be patched from it at the next revision. Where an amendment needs a chapter or decision change, it is also listed in section 2 ("Spec changes required"); the executors follow the amendment, not the unchanged chapter text, until the owner rules otherwise.

**Notation.** `Pn-Tm` is task m of plan n (`P5a-T3`, `P5b-T0`). "Owner" is the task that implements the amendment. "Adapt" lists the other tasks whose code or tests must change (they are small edits, mostly a use line, a field or an expected string). Each decision carries one sentence "Why" naming the rule or decision it follows.

**Groups.** G-A types and signatures, G-B file and line formats, G-C behaviour conflicts, G-D dependency and ordering. 72 amendments in all. Section 1.5 lists the plan issues that are accepted exactly as the plan proposed them (no change elsewhere).
